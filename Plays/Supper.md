## Characters
John, the eldest son.  
Jim, the middle-sized son.  
Bill, the son-of-a-gun.  
Also parents, foods, table linen, etc.

BILL  
If you do, I'll kill you, I think.

JIM  
You can't kill me, bees I'll get up on the roof and hide.

BILL  
But I'll climb on the roof and get you; that's what I'll do Mr. Jimmie.

JIM  
But when I see you coming up on the roof, I'll jump off the roof.

JOHN

BILL

JIM

If you say one more word to me, I'll kill you, Mr. Jimmie. And Mr. John and Mr. Daddy and Mr. baby brother.

JIM  
O, nobody could kill baby brother. He's too little.

BILL

JIM

JIM  
O, bake yourself!

JOHN

BILL
