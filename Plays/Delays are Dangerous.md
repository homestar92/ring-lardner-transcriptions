# Delays Are Dangerous

## Characters  
William Gleason, a Trusted Lieutenant.  
Harry Lord, a Noble Captain.  
Ralph Plague, a Chicago Bug.  
George Dime, a Bellhop.

### SCENE I
(The lobby of a California hotel. It is evening. GLEASON is seated in a comfortable chair digesting his supper. Enter LORD.)

LORD  
All organized, Kid. But there's only four of us, not counting you.

GLEASON  
Who've ya got?

LORD  
Scotty and Ping and Joe and myself.

GLEASON  
Good bunch outside o' Ping. Go ahead, Harry, and I'll be up in a couple o' minutes. Got plenty o' cards?

LORD  
We'll get some new ones. Hurry it along. Room 324.

GLEASON  
All right, I'll be right there. I want to buy a couple o' stamps.

(Exit LORD. GLEASON walks toward the cigar counter. DIME accosts him.)

DIME  
Mr. Gleason? Gentleman here to see you. 

GLEASON  
What's his name?

DIME  
Mr. Plague from Chicago.

GLEASON  
I don't know no Plague.

DIME  
There he stands, by that pillar.

(MR. PLAGUE advances and DIME exits.)

PLAGUE  
Mr. Gleason?

GLEASON  
Yeh. What can I do for you?

PLAGUE  
My name is Plague, Mr. Gleason. I guess you don't remember me.

GLEASON  
I guess not.

PLAGUE  
I'm from old Chi. I met you oncet out to the ball park. O'Neill introduced me to ya.

GLEASON  
O, yeh.

PLAGUE  
I thought I'd look ya up.

GLEASON  
Yeh, glad to see ya.

(Enter DIME.)

DIME  
Mr. Gleason, Mr. Lord says they're waitin' for ya.

GLEASON  
Tell him I'll be right up.

(Exit DIME.)

PLAGUE  
I won't keep ya long.

GLEASON  
That's all right.

PLAGUE  
Well, what about Walsh?

GLEASON  
He's all right.

PLAGUE  
Think he'll be as good as ever?

GLEASON  
Sure.

(Enter DIME.)

DIME  
Mr. Gleason, Mr. Lord says they're waitin' for ya.

GLEASON  
I'll be right along.

(Exit DIME.)

PLAGUE  
What d'ya think o' the Federal league?

GLEASON  
Pretty hard to tell yet.

PLAGUE  
They're spendin' a lot o' dough.

GLEASON  
Yeh.

PLAGUE  
How's it come you didn't go with 'em?

GLEASON  
Me? They don't want me. They're lookin' for stars.

PLAGUE  
I heard they was after ya.

GLEASON  
Nothin' to it.

PLAGUE  
Any o' the young fellahs look good?

GLEASON  
Sure.

PLAGUE  
What about Roth?

GLEASON  
Looks pretty good.

(Enter DIME.)

DIME  
Mr. Gleason, Mr. Lord says "Hurry up."

GLEASON  
Comin' right up.

(Exit DIME.)

PLAGUE  
Mebbe you want to go somewheres.

GLEASON  
That's all right.

PLAGUE  
How's Comiskey?

GLEASON  
He's all right, I guess.

PLAGUE  
I heard he was pretty sick.

GLEASON  
Nothin' to it.

PLAGUE  
Goin' to hold onto Bodie?

GLEASON  
Sure.

PLAGUE  
That Chappell turned out to be a kind o'  lemon didn't he?

GLEASON  
He's a good ball player.

PLAGUE  
He didn't hig very good when he broke in. 

GLEASON  
Nobody hits all the time.

(Enter DIME.)

DIME  
Mr. Gleason, Mr. Lord wants to know if you're comin'.

GLEASON  
Tell him I don't know. No, tell him I'll be along pretty quick.

(GLEASON winks at DIME as latter exits.)

PLAGUE  
Am I keepin' ya?

GLEASON  
That's all right.

PLAGUE  
Where's Callahan at?

GLEASON  
Went to church, I guess.

PLAGUE  
Do ball players like him?

GLEASON  
Sure. Why not?

PLAGUE  
Oh, nothin'. Who's goin' to win the pennant?

GLEASON  
What pennant?

PLAGUE  
'Merican league.

GLEASON  
Well, it's between eight teams.

PLAGUE  
You fellahs got any chancet?

GLEASON  
No, we've give up. We ain't even goin' to try.

PLAGUE  
I don't think the Athaletics can come back.

GLEASON  
Where from?

PLAGUE  
I mean, I don't think they can repeat.

(Enter DIME.)

DIME  
Call for Mr. Plague.

PLAGUE  
Who wants me?

GLEASON  
Damfi know.

PLAGUE  
Well, see you later.

(Exit PLAGUE.)

GLEASON  
Yes, you will.

DIME  
Better duck while the goin's good.

GLEASON  
Here's somethin for your trouble.

DIME  
Thanks.

(DIME exits. GLEASON starts toward elevator, but meets LORD.)

GLEASON  
What's the matter?

LORD  
I couldn't keep 'em any longer. They wouldn't stand for a four-handed game.

GLEASON  
Where are they at?

LORD  
Scotty and Joe went to a show and Ping went after a sandwich, I guess. What was the matte with you?

GLEASON  
That ⸻! If I was drinkin' I'd take some absinthe.

LORD  
Who's this guy coming?

(Exit GLEASON.)
