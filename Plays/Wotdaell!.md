# Wotdaell
## A Play in Three Scenes

### Characters
C. W. Murf, a Wonder.  
Vic Saier, a Complete Letter Writer.  
Reporters, Office Attachés, etc.

(Scene 1—Mr. Murf's office, one day last week. Discovered are Mr. Murf, attachés, and the reporters.)

MR. MURF  
Well, whats new in the Federal league?

A REPORTER  
The St. Louis Feds are after Vic Saier.

MR. MURF  
Well, I've got a better first baseman than Vic Saier.

AN ATTACHÉ  
That's right.

ANOTHER ATTACHÉ  
That's right.

ALL REPORTERS  
Good night, Nurse.

(Scene 2—A, or The, Hotel at Lansing. Time, last Sunday. Discovered, Mr. Murf. Enter Vic Saier.)

MR. MURF  
Well, Vic, I hear you have some offers from the Federal league.

SAIER  
Yes, sir.

MR. MURF  
For how much?

SAIER  
So-and-so.

MR. MURF  
Well, I'll give you So-and-so a year if you'll sign a three-year contract.

SAIER  
All right.

(Scene 3—Mr. Murf's office, last Monday. Discovered, Mr. Murf and Attachés. Enter Reporters.)

MR. MURF  
Well, boys, I've received Saier's signed contract and a typewritten letter from Saier. He wrote the letter and mailed the contract Saturday. The letter says he will stick to the Cubs, "an organization which the public respects."

ALL REPORTERS  
Sounds just like Vic.

A REPORTER  
Where were you Sunday?

MR. MURF  
I went up to Lansing and signed Saier.

A REPORTER  
Did you meet his stenographer?

ANOTHER REPORTER  
Did Saier write the letter and mail the contract before or after you went to Lansing and signed him?

MR. MURF  
I have nothing to say.
