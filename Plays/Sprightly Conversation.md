## Sprightly Conversation

("The exclusive Casino club is going to open its doors to as many jackies and private soldiers as can be accommodated for a series of dances, beginning March 23. Twenty Junior league girls have been invited to furnish the sprightly conversation for hte young men of th earmy and navy and the week end parties."—The Trib.)

### Characters
A Jackie.  
A Girl.  
Another Jackie.  
Another Girl.  

### Scene 1.—The club.

A GIRL  
(approaching a jackie.) Wouldn't you like to dance this with me?

A JACKIE  
Yes, ma'am.

(They dance.)

GIRL  
Are you in the navy?

JACKIE  
Yes.

(They dance.)

GIRL  
Pardon me.

JACKIE  
That was my fault.

(They dance.)

GIRL  
Do you like it in the navy?

JACKIE  
Pretty good.

GIRL  
How long do you think this war's going ot last?

JACKIE  
Hard to tell.

(They dance.)

GIRL  
Do you live here in Chicago?

JACKIE  
No.

(They dance.)

GIRL  
You say you don't live here?

JACKIE  
No.

(Encore.)

GIRL  
Where do you come from?

JACKIE  
Me? I come from Fort Wayne?

GIRL  
Oh, yes.

(They dance.)

GIRL  
How do you like it in the army?

JACKIE  
I'm in the navy.

GIRL  
Oh, yes.

(They dance.)

GIRL  
Do you live here in Chicago?

JACKIE  
No, I come from Fort Wayne.

GIRL  
Oh, yes. I believe we came through there once on the way from Pasadena.

JACKIE  
Oh, yes.

(They dance.)

GIRL  
How often do you soldiers get leave of absence?

JACKIE  
I'm in the navy.

GIRL  
Oh, yes. Do you get much leave of absence?

JACKIE  
Oh, quite a bit.

GIRL  
Who's colonel of your company?

(They dance.)

GIRL  
Do you live here in Chicago?

JACKIE  
No. I come from Indiana.

GIRL  
Oh, yes.

(Encore.)

GIRL  
How do you like it in the army?

JACKIE  
Pretty good.

GIRL  
Who's general of your battery?

JACKIE  
Major Pershing.

GIRL  
How long do you think this war's going to last?

JACKIE  
I think it'll be over this week.

GIRL  
Oh, do you! Why?

(Orchestra stops.)

### Scene 2.—The club.

GIRL  
Weren't they just too funny!

ANOTHER GIRL  
Weren't they just screaming!

### Scene 3.—The 11:05.

JACKIE  
Did you have a good time?

ANOTHER JACKIE  
Shut up! I want to sleep.

JACKIE  
Oh, Min!
