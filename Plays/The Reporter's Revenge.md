# The Reporter's Revenge; or, the Thirst for Knowledge

## SCENE I

A place in the loop. Afternoon. SHANO and JULIUS, mix artists; MARTY, a citizen, and the REPORTER.

REPORTER  
What do you think of the Federal league?

SHANO  
Who? Me?

REPORTER  
Yes. You.

SHANO  
I impatic'ly don't think nothin' of it. I'm with the Nationals.

MARTY  
Ditto.

REPORTER  
What does that mean?

SHANO  
What does what mean?

REPORTER  
"Ditto."

SHANO  
That means he thinks the same as me. "Ditto" dessinates one distinction.

REPORTER  
(To JULIUS.) What do YOU think of the Federal league?

JULIUS  
It's goin' to be a big thing if Shano don' think nothin' of it.

## SCENE II
A place in the loop. Same afternoon. BILLY JONES, bellhop, and the REPORTER.

REPORTER  
What do you think of the Federal league?

BILLY  
All right, boss; all right.

REPORTER  
Do you mean you think it's all right?

BILLY  
Dat's all right boss. I'm for 'em.

## SCENE III
A place in the loop. Same afternoon. A TRAFFIC COP and the REPORTER.

REPORTER  
Well, it's pretty windy.

COP  
Yes, but it looks as though it might warm up.

REPORTER  
Pretty near time.

COP  
Yes. They'll be playin' ball pretty soon now.

REPORTER  
What do you think of the Federal league?

COP  
Me? O, as far as I'm concerned, they might as well be three or four leagues as one. I can't ge tout to the games. If the people wants 'em, all right. I won't stop 'em.

REPORTER  
Do you think the Feds will stick?

COP  
Do I think they'll stick? Well, they'll stick as far as I'm concerned. I won't stop 'em. Toot, toot.

### SCENE V
A Place in the Loop. Same Afternoon. JOHNNIE, Mix Artist, and the REPORTER.

REPORTER  
What do you think of hte Federal league?

JOHNNIE  
Well, it's a good deal like a horse.

REPORTER  
How's that?

JOHNNIE  
That's the way I look at it; it's a good deal like a horse.

REPORTER  
How do you mean?

JOHNNIE  
Well, you can't never tell nothin' about a horse till you see what kind of a track he's runnin' on.

REPORTER  
What's that got to do with it?

JOHNNIE  
Got a whole lot to do with it. The Feds is just like some horse. You can't tell nothin' about 'em till you see what kind of track he's runnin' on. They're just like a horse.

REPORTER  
Well, that's five horses. You must be stuck by this time.

### SCENE VI
A Place in the Loop. Same afternoon. A LADY CASHIER and the REPORTER.

REPORTER  
What do you think of the Federal league?

LADY  
What do I know about it? You're tryin' to kid somebody.

REPORTER  
No, it's on the square. I want it for the paper.

LADY  
Don't you put my name in the paper.

REPORTER  
How do you spell it?

(She spells it.)

REPORTER  
Now, what do you think of the Federal league?

LADY  
I don't know anything about it I'm sore at them for what they did to Johnnie Evvers. I'm a fan for whatever team he's on. Boston Red Sox, isn't it?

### SCENE VII
A Place in the Loop. Same afternoon. KENNEDY and FITCH, Mix Artists and the REPORTER

REPORTER  
What do you think of the Federal league?

KENNEDY  
Just a big gamble. That's the way I look at it. Just a big gamble.

REPORTER  
(to FITCH.) What do YOU think of the Federal league?

(No answer.)

### SCENE VIII
A Place in the Loop. Same afternoon. HAPPY, a waiter, and the REPORTER

REPORTER  
What do you think of the Federal league?

HAPPY  
Well, I don't know what to think. If they got plenty o' money and is willin' to spend it I don't see no reason why they shouldn't ought to make it a go but still from all I can learn Ban Johnson and them is goin' to make a hot fight and they prob'ly got more money if they put it all together but still if the Fed'rals stick together and fight and give the public good baseball I don't see why they shouldn't ought to make good right off hte reel if they play the right kind o' ball and play high class ball and get the crowds out but still you can't tell nothin' about it.

### SCENE IX
A Place in the Loop. Same afternoon. BEN, Mix Artist, and the REPORTER.

REPORTER  
What do you think of the Federal league?

BEN  
Wot you ast me for? You know I don't know nuttin' about it. I hear 'em ravin' round here, but I can't get no sense to it. Baseball or no baseball, wot do I care. I'm no criterion.

### SCENE X
A Place in the Loop. Same afternoon. JOE, a Neway, and the REPORTER

REPORTER  
How's everything?

JOE  
Rotten, rotten.

REPORTER  
What do you think of the Federal league?

JOE  
Do you want ot know what I think o' the Fed'ral league?

REPORTER  
Yes.

JOE  
Well, I ain't thought much about it. If it's a go, it's a go, and if it ain't, it ain't. That's all they is to it. Anyway, they ain't neither one o' the three teams here wort adam. The Sox never was no good and the Cubs gave away all the men they had. McGraw and Coney Mack's got the only teams and if the Cubs was to get hold o' somethin' good, COney Mack'd take 'em away from it. Murphy made his big mistake when he let Chance go. I wouldn't go to none o' their games if you hired me. They're all a bunch o' burglars.

### SCENE XI
A Place in the Loop. Same afternoon. CLIFF, a Writer, and the REPORTER

REPORTER  
What do you think of the Federal league?

CLIFF  
What is the Federal league?

REPORTER  
I don't know.

CLIFF  
I've got a better question that that for you.

REPORTER  
Shoot.

CLIFF  
What'll you have?
