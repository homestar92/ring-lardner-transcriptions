# Small Talk at Breakfast

## Characters
John  
Jim  
We

---

JOHN  
What color is my pajamas?

WE  
White and blue.

JOHN  
What color is my p'liceman's suit?

WE  
Blue.

JIM  
What color's mine?

JOHN  
You haven't any p'liceman's suit.

JIM  
I HAVE.

JOHN  
What color's Jim's pajamas?

WE  
White and blue.

JIM  
Just like Yohn's.

JOHN  
What color's Jim's eyes?

WE  
Brown.

JIM  
What color's Yohn's eyes?

WE  
Brown.

JIM  
Yust like Dim's.

JOHN  
But my hair's different. What color's my hair?

WE  
Dark brown.

JOHN  
What color's Jim's hair?

WE  
Yellow.

JIM  
Oh, Yah.

---

JOHN  
What color's people's faces?

WE  
Different colors.

JOHN  
What color's my face?

WE  
Whitish and brownish and flesh color.

JOHN  
What color's Jim's face?

WE  
White and pink.

JIM  
Oh, yah.

JOHN  
Is everybody's faces different colors?

WE  
No. Yours is about the same color as lots of people's.

JIM  
Me, too.

JOHN  
Is anybody's face red?

WE  
Yes, some people's.

JOHN  
Is my face red?

WE  
Sometimes.

JOHN  
Is Jim's?

WE  
Sometimes.

JOHN  
Are Indians?

WE  
Yes.

JIM  
Oh, yah.

---

JOHN  
Where do Indians live?

WE  
Out west.

JOHN  
I know which the two coldest are.

WE  
What?

JOHN  
West and south's the warmest and east and north's the coldest.

JIM  
Oh, yah.

JOHN  
West is California and Yellowstones Park and east is New York and south's the South Pole and north's the North Pole.

JIM  
Who's the pole?

JOHN  
It isn't the pole. It's the North Pole and South Pole.

JIM  
Oh, yah.

JOHN  
Look! Jim's spilling his mile.

JIM  
I am not.

JOHN  
It's spilled all over. He spilled it on the floor.

JIM  
It spilled itself.

---

JOHN  
What's today?

WE  
Friday.

JOHN  
What comee after Friday?

WE  
Saturday.

JOHN  
What comes after Friday?

WE  
Sunday.

JOHN  
And does Doc-a-Yak come Sunday?

WE  
Yes.

JOHN  
Why don't Doc-a-Yak come every day.

WE  
Once a week is a great plenty.

JIM  
Oh, yah.
