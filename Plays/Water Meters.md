## Water Meters

### Characters
Papa.  
Mamma.  
Little Jack.  
Little Jill.  
Hilda, the Maid.  
Place—Home. Time—Breakfast

MAMMA  
I wonder what's the matter with the goldfish. They act so foolish!

JACK  
Somepin's the matter with 'em all right. I seen—

MAMMA  
Saw!

JACK  
I saw that great biggest one kiss the next biggest one and the next biggest one started to chase him, but felled down.

PAPA  
Have you changed their beer lately?

MAMMA  
Well, I was going to yesterday, but I found we didn't have any left in the house, so I freshened the bowl up with musty.

PAPA  
That explains it! It's too strong for them.

MAMMA  
Well, I don't know what to do. The beer man won't be here till noon and the way they're carrying on now they'll break the bowl before that time.

PAPA  
O, no. Chances are they'll go to sleep pretty soon.

JILL  
Mamma, kin I have a piece of candy this mornin'?

MAMMA  
No, indeed; It makes you thirsty.

JILL  
But there's a drop of water left in the sponge.

MAMMA  
No, there isn't. Your father used it to shave with.

JILL  
O, papa! It was mine!

PAPA  
Well, I can't help it. We're out of buttermilk and that brandy hurts my face.

MAMMA  
Hilda, what did you make this coffee with? It's awfully weak!

HILDA  
I know, ma'am. I had to use some of last night's gravy. I seen—

JACK  
Mamma, Hilda says "seen."

MAMMA  
Never mind, Jack.

HILDA  
I seen they was only half a pint of wine left and the washwoman will need that.

MAMMA  
We must order another barrel of wine today.

HILDA  
Yes'm.

MAMMA  
And Hilda, don't forget to heat a pail of Worcestershire sauce for the children's baths.

JACK  
O, look! It's rainin'!

PAPA  
By George, it is! Are all the tubs out?

JILL  
O, goody! Maybe now I kin have a piece of candy!
