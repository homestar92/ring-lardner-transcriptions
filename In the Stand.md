# In the Stand

(First Act.)

LADY (as Dolan comes to bat for the first time)  
Who's that?

ESCORT  
Dolan.

LADY  
O, yes; Mike Doolan.

(Second Act.)

LADY (as Dolan comes to bat for the second time)  
Who's that?

ESCORT  
Mike Dolan.

LADY  
O, Yes, I saw he and his wife play at the Grand in Pittsburgh.

(Third Act.)

ESCORT (third time DOlan comes to bat)  
Mike Doolan again.

LADY  
He used to be a good batter.

ESCORT  
He better go on a nickel stage now.

LADY  
That's what he's been doing all winter.
