## Characters
A Registrant.  
An Official.

(REGISTRANT enters and approaches desk.)

REGISTRANT  
(Holding out questionnaire) Is dis here ware you see about this here?

OFFICIAL  
What about it?

REGISTRANT  
I taught dis was ware you come.

OFFICIAL  
Have you filled it out?

REGISTRANT  
(Hands questionnaire to OFFICIAL) Yeh, I come to see about dis here questioner. I ain't got it filled in, see? I got it wrote up down to ware says somethin' about a key. I don't know wat de hell about no key. I ain't no locksmith.

---

OFFICIAL  
(Looks at Series 1 on questionnaire) Well, you've started right.

REGISTRANT  
Yeh, but I don't know wat de hell about no key.

OFFICIAL  
Well, what do you do for a living?

REGISTRANT  
I work for my brudder. Wrestle cans.

OFFICIAL  
What's your brother's business?

REGISTRANT  
Garbage.

OFFICIAL  
What's his name?

REGISTRANT  
Jack Sweet.

OFFICIAL  
You've got your name down as Willis.

REGISTRANT  
Yeh, de old woman got mert twicet. She mert my old man and soon's he died she mert dis here Sweet dat already been mert couple times and got dis here Jack.

OFFICIAL  
Where do you work?

REGISTRANT  
Up an alley most de time.

---

OFFICIAL  
I mean, where is Sweet's place of business?

REGISTRANT  
Tirty-tree-o-fife Wes Matson.

OFFICIAL  
Have you ever been a soldier?

REGISTRANT  
Soldier hell.

OFFICIAL  
What branch of the army would you rather go into?

REGISTRANT  
Oh, if I got to go I go wit d'Mericans.

OFFICIAL  
Have you ever been to school?

REGISTRANT  
Nah.

OFFICIAL  
What languages do you speak well, English, French, German?

REGISTRANT  
Just English.
