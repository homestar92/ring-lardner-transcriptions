# Anything Else?

## Characters
The Doorman.  
A Man from the East.
Scene—The Reception Room.

DOORMAN  
Did you wish to see somebody?

MAN  
I want to see the sporting editor.

DOORMAN  
He won't be down until this evening.

MAN  
Well, maybe you can tell me what I want to know.

DOORMAN  
What is it?

MAN  
Detroit had a pitcher named Clauss and they let him go.

DOORMAN  
Well?

MAN  
Where is he at?

DOORMAN  
I can't tell you. You'd have to see somebody in the sporting department.

MAN  
I think he went to Jackson in the Southern Michigan League.

DOORMAN  
I don't know, I'm sure.

MAN  
I'm from the east.

DOORMAN  
Is that so?

MAN  
If I should go into an eastern newspaper office they'd tell me in a minute.

DOORMAN  
Tell you what?

MAN  
What team Clauss is with. He's a friend of mine.

DOORMAN  
Well, I'm sorry, but I can't tell you.

MAN  
Can a man go from here to Kalamazoo by trolley?

DOORMAN  
I don't know. I guess you could find out by looking in the railway guide.

MAN  
Well, I guess I'll look in a railway guide.

DOORMAN  Yes.

MAN  
Suppose I find out I can't get there by trolley?

DOORMAN  
Well?

MAN  
I'd have to stay here all night.

DOORMAN  
You could take a train.

MAN  
No, I'd have to stay here.

DOORMAN  
Well?

MAN  
Where's the headquarters of the K. P. in Chicago?

DOORMAN  
I don't know. You could probably find out by looking in a directory.

MAN  
So you can't tell me where Clauss is at?

DOORMAN  
No.

MAN  
Well, much obliged to you.
