## CHARACTERS.
Le Père.  
La Mère.  
John, the eldest son.  
Jim, the middle-sized son.  
Bill, the son-of-a-gun  
Anonymous, the youngest son.  
Scene—The boudoir of Anonymous

JOHN  
My littlest finger is bigger than his biggest finger.

JIM  
Lookut! Mine, too.

BILL  
I'm bigger than a king.

JOHN  
I want him to be named Joe.

LE PÈRE  
That would make too many J's in the family.

JIM  
My name spells a J and John's, too.

BILL  
My name's Mr. Bill, I think.

JIM  
Why can't she talk?

JOHN  
She! It's a boy.

JIM  
I mean a boy. Why can't he talk?

LA MÈRE  
He's too little.

JIM  
But if he's too little to talk, why can he cry?

JOHN  
Babies can always cry.

LE PÈRE  
And some seven-year-old boys, too.

BILL  
I never cry, I think.

JIM  
Oh, you do, too, cry!

BILL  
I do not cry, Mr. Jimmie. If you say I cry, I'll kill you, I think.

JIM  
Oh, kill yourself!

JOHN  
Why can't we name him Joe or Robert?

LA MÈRE  
Because he looks as if he wanted to be a Nickie.

JIM  
Oh, Nickie! There's nobody Nickie.

BILL  
Mrs. Bill, I think her name is.

JIM  
Oh, her yourself; It's a brother.

BILL  
If you say it's a brother, I'll kill you, Mr. Jimmie.

JOHN  
He's our little brother, aren't you, baby?

ANONYMOUS  
(Looks bored and goes to sleep.)
