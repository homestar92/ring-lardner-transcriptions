# What Do You Want?
## Play in One Act.

Scene—The Office. Time— Thursday Afternoon.

(The editor is sitting at his desk. There enters a middle aged gentleman of apparently sound body.)

GENT  
Sporting Editor?

ED.  
No. He won't be in till tonight.

GENT  
Could you tell me where I might find Sport Donnelly? He's an old football player.

ED.  
Yes, I've heard of him. If I were you I'd call up Ed Smith at Main 1050.

GENT  
Would you mind calling him up for me? I'm not very good at talking over the phone.

(The editor complies and returns to his desk.)

ED.  
He stops over at the C. A. A.

GENT  
Thanks. (He starts out, hesitates, and returns to the desk.) Would you mind calling up and finding out whether he is there?

(The editor complies and returns to his desk)

ED.  
They say you'd probably find him at the I. A. C.

GENT  
Thanks. (He starts out , hesitates, and returns to the desk.) Would you mind—

(The editor not only starts out but goes through with it.)
