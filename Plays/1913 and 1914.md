# 1913

## Characters
Butch Swatem, a Ath-a-lete.  
A. Magnate, Owner of the Club.  
Scene—A. Magnate's Office.  

(A. MAGNATE is seated at his desk. BUTCH enters from the reception room.)

BUTCH  
How are you, Mr. Magnate?

(MAGNATE opens and reads thirty letters, smokes a few cigars, and then picks up a magazine.)

BUTCH  
How are you, Mr. Magnate?

MAGNATE  
I'm busy.

BUTCH  
I won't take more than a minute of your time. I want to see about signin' up.

MAGNATE  
O, see my secretary. I haven't no time to monkey with your ball players.

BUTCH  
I already seen your secretary and he said I'd have to see you.

(MAGNATE reads that month's installment of a serial story.)

MAGNATE  
You still here?

BUTCH  
Yeh. I want to see about signing' up.

MAGNATE  
Well, you've got your contract, haven't ya?

BUTCH  
Yeh.

MAGNATE  
Well, w'y don't ya sign and be done with it?

BUTCH  
I ain't satisfied with it.

MAGNATE  
W'at is it you ain't satisfied with?

BUTCH  
The salary.

MAGNATE  
Well, say, if you don't like that salary go take a jump in the lake. I havn't no time to talk to ya.

BUTCH  
I won't never play no ball for this money.

MAGNATE  
I should worry. If you won't play ball I'll get somebody else.

BUTCH  
You can't get nobody like me.

MAGNATE  
I don't want nobody like you.

BUTCH  
I'm through with you guys.

MAGNATE  
Beat it. I've got a lot o' work to do.

BUTCH  
Ain't you goin' to do nothin' about this hear contract?

MAGNATE  
Will I have to call a officer?

BUTCH  
Yes, call a officer.

MAGNATE  
Well, I will call a officer.

BUTCH  
Yeh, go ahead and call a offier.

MAGNATE  
Well, I will.

BUTCH  
Ain't you goin' to give me w'at I want?

MAGNATE  
Listen now. I'm through with ya. If you don't like it you can do the other thing. See? Now, beat it!

# 1914

## Characters
Butch Swatem, a Ath-a-lete.  
A. Magnate, Owner of the Club.  
Scene—A. Magnate's Office.  

(A. MAGNATE is seated at his desk. BUTCH enters the reception room. A. MAGNATE jumps out of his chair.)

MAGNATE  
How are ya, Mr. Swatem? How are ya, Butch? You don't mind if I call you Butch, do ya?

BUTCH  
Mr. Swatem to me, please.

MAGNATE  
I beg your pardon, Mr. Swatem. What can I do for ya, old boy?

BUTCH  
I come about this here contract you sent me. I won't sign it, see?

MAGNATE  
Well, well, I don't blame ya. It was a clerical error. I won't sign it, see?

BUTCH  
Why don't you git a secretary that knows somethin'?

MAGNATE  
I'll discharge him tomorrow.

BUTCH  
How about this here contract?

MAGNATE  
Well, my dear mr. Swatem, fill it in to suit yourself.

BUTCH  
All right.

(He fills it in to suit himself.)

MAGNATE  
There, that's all right. I hope that's satisfactory to you.

BUTCH  
This here is all right now. The contract's all right. I ain't got no kick comin' on that.

MAGNATE  
Well, what else can I do for ya?

BUTCH  
You're chewin' gum, ain't ya? Cut it out.

(MAGNATE removes the gum from his mouth.)

MAGNATE  
There. I beg your pardon. I didn't know you objected to it.

BUTCH  
Where did ya get that there fountain pen?

MAGNATE  
O, that? That was a present to me.

BUTCH  
Say, I want that. See? I want it.

MAGNATE  
Sure. All right. Take it.

BUTCH  
Thanks. W'at are ya goin' to do tonight?

MAGNATE  
Me? I'm going home to supper.

BUTCH  
That's fine. I'll go with ya.

MAGNATE  
Well, that's great. We'll be glad to have ya.

BUTCH  
What are ya goin' to have?

MAGNATE  
I think it's pork chops.

BUTCH  
I don't like 'em. See? Get somethin' else.

MAGNATE  
All right, all right. I'll call up the missus.

BUTCH  
Well, so long. I'll come round about five bells.

MAGNATE  
Wait a minute, Mr. Swatem. You got some dust on your shoes.

(He produces a silk handkerchief.)
