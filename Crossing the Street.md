# Characters

C. Albert Comiskey, an Old Roman.  
Ed Walsh, a Former Pitcher.  
Wake, a Visitor.  
Harry Grab, a Hustler.

Scene 1—A row in the grandstand, Afternoon. Discovered sitting side by side are Ed Walsh, C. Albert Comiskey, and Wake.

C. ALBERT COMISKEY  
Look at the little center fielder. He was playing right there for Daley, a left handed batter. Now Walsh is up and he hasn't moved. He must think they're brothers and always hit to the same spot.

ED WALSH  
Pipe Chase. Watch him now. If he gets two strikes, he'll take the gum off his cap.

WAKE  
What's the idea?

E. W.  
Just superstitious. There. See? Didn't do any good. He whiffed him. Now tomorrow, after he gets two strikes, he'll leave the gum on his cap.

C. A. C.  
We ought to finish them here. They look about as confident as a bunch of schoolgirls.

E. W.  
Shawkey tried to stop that one with his nose. They're gone.

C. A. C.  
One run. We ought to have had a thousand. I can't sit here and watch that. Let's go across the street. Coming, Ed?

E. W.  
No, thanks.

(Exeunt, all but E. W.)

Scene 2—A thirst parlor. Enter Wake and C. A. C.

WAKE  
Still water, please.

C. A. C.  
Cleveland's got two. That ought to scare 'em some more. All we need is a couple more runs and they'll die. Let's go across the street.

(Exeunt.)

Scene 3—Another row  in the stand. Enter Wake and C. A. C.

C. A. C.  
Why didn't he throw it home? He must have attended a school where they taught everybody to relay.

WAKE  
He wasn't very far out.

C. A. C.  
He throws with both arms. He'd better try the other one and give that one a rest. Let's go across the street.

(Exeunt.)

Scene 4—Another spot in the T. Parlor. Enter C. A. C. and Wake.

WAKE  
Still water, please.

C. A. C.  
You're liable to get drowned.

WAKE  
Boston got three.

C. A. C.  
Well, Cleveland'll Catch 'em. Let's go across the street.

(Exeunt.)

Scene 5—Another row in the grandstand. Enter C. A. C. and Wake.

C. A. C.  
Well, Connolly ain't paying any attention to him now. He's talking to the boy with the ball bag. He's got to talk to somebody. Let's go across the street.

(Exeunt.)

Scene 6—Another spot in the T. Parlor. Enter C. A. C. and Wake.

WAKE  
Still water, please.

C. A. C.  
Five runs. Good heavens. They got enough, didn't they? That's too bad ffor Benz. He's just lost a couple of tough ones before this.

(Enter Harry Grab.)

H. GRAB.  
They got five.

C. A. C.  
How did they get them, Harry?

H. G.  
Schang hit a triple with the bases full.

C. A. C.  
Well, was there five men on? Triple, eh? His name oughta been Whang or Bang. We don't get any triples with the bases full.

H. G.  
Nothing, thanks. I got to go back.

WAKE  
Still water, please.

(One hour elapses. Enter H. Grab.)

H. GRAB  
It's raining outside.

C. A. C.  
WHy coultn'd it 'a rained at 3 o'clock? Too late now.

H. GRAB  
No, thanks. I got to go.

WAKE  
Cigar this time.

(Curtain.)
