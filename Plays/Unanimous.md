## Characters
Mr. B., just back from France.  
Mr. McC., just back from France.  
Mr. H., just back from France.  
Editorial Writers, Business Managers, Circulation Managers, Auditors, Southern Cartoonists, Journalists, Citizens, etc.

## ACT 1
(Luncheon at the Overset Club. Mr. B., Editorial Writers, Business Managers, Auditors, Circulation Managers, and Southern Cartoonists are seated round the board.)

AN ED. WRITER  
Well, Tiff, are you glad to get back?

MR. B.  
You bet.

A SOUTHERN CARTOONIST  
Did you have an interesting trip?

MR. B.  
Very interesting.

AN ED. WRITER  
Did you see any of the devastated territory?

MR. B.  
Yes, quite a bi tof it.

AN AUDITOR  
What was the outstanding feature, the feature that impressed you the most of the whole situation?

MR. B.  
Well—

AN ED. WRITER  
Did you see Gibbons?

MR. B.  
Yes.

A CIRCULATION MANAGER  
How is Paris?

MR. B.  
Some town.

A CIRCULATION MANAGER  
Same old Paris, is it?

AN ED. WRITER  
How do the French feel toward the Americans?

MR. B.  
Well—

## ACT 2
(Luncheon at the Overset Club. One week later. Mr. McC., Editorial Writers, Business Managers, Auditors, Circulation Managers, and Southern Cartoonists are seated around the board.)

AN ED. WRITER  Well, John, are you glad to get back?

MR. McC.  
Yes, indeed.

A SOUTHERN CARTOONIST  
Did you see any of the devastated territory?

MR. McC.  
A good deal of it.

A SOUTHERN CARTOONIST  
How did it look?

MR. McC.  
Well, kind of devastated.

AN AUDITOR  
What feature impressed you most of the whole situation?

MR. McC.  
Well—

A CIRCULATION MANAGER  
How is Paris?

MR. McC.  
About the same.

A CIRCULATION MANAGER  
The same old Paris.

AN ED. WRITER  
Did you get to fly over the channel?

MR. McC.  
No.

## ACT 3
(Afternoon in the Local Room. One day later. Mr. H., Editorial Writers, Journalists, Citizens, etc.)

AN ED. WRITER  
Well, Perce, are you glad to get back?

MR. H.  
I'll say so.

A CITIZEN  
Did you have an interesting trip?

MR. H.  
I'll say I did.

A JOURNALIST  
How's Paris?

MR. H.  
All right.

AN ED. WRITER  
Did you see Gibbons?

MR. H.  
Yes.

A CITIZEN  
Did you seen any of the devastated territory?

MR. H.  
I'll say I did.
