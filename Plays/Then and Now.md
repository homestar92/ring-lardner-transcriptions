## Characters
A Person.  
An Automobile Salesman.

## ACT 1.
(The action takes place on Michigan avenue. Time, 1914)

A PERSON  
(entering the salesroom of the Eclipse Motor Company) I wanted to know if you had a car for sale.

SALESMAN  
(falls in a faint; recovers) Yes, $1,200. When must you have it?

PERSON  
In ten minutes.

SALESMAN  
Don't be impatient. I'll have it for you in five.

PERSON  
But I want to trade in a car I already have. It's an Elite.

SALESMAN  
What did you pay for it new?

PERSON  
$1,100.

SALESMAN  
We'll allow you $1,300 on the new purchase.

## ACT 2.
(Michigan Avenue. Time, 1919.)

PERSON  
I'd like to buy a new car.

SALESMAN  
(yawns).

PERSON  
I've got the money to pay for it.

SALESMAN  
(falls asleep).

PERSON  
Do you want to sell me a car or not?

SALESMAN  
Not.

PERSON  
What's the price of your new Eclipse?

SALESMAN  
Fifty-three hunred.

PERSON  
Is it any better than the 1914 Eclipse?

SALESMAN  
What of it?

PERSON  
Well, I've got to have a new car.

SALESMAN  
Go get it.

PERSON  
I want to get it here.

SALESMAN  
O, you want to buy it here? Sure, we'll sell you one.

PERSON  
When can I get it?

SALESMAN  
In October, 1923.

PERSON  
And can I trade in this Elite I've got?

SALESMAN  
O, yes (falls asleep).

## ACT 3.
(Eight days later.)

SALESMAN  
(waking up) What did you want?

PERSON  
I wanted to trade in my old Elite for a new Eclipse and pay you  the difference. How much will you give me for my old Elite and when can I have the new Eclipse?

SALESMAN  
(Falls asleep).
