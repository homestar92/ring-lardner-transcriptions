## CHARACTERS.
Le Père.  
John, the eldest son.  
Jim, the middle-sized son.  
Bill, the son-of-a-gun  

## ACT 1
(Le Père's room.)

JOHN  
(Outside)  Can I come in a minute? (He comes in) I just wanted to know if you've got any neckties.

LE PÈRE  
Very few.

JOHN  
Any old ones I could have?

LE PÈRE  
I'll look. (He looks) Here are three I don't want.

JOHN  
Thanks. I want to wear them. (He goes out with the neckties.)

## ACT 2
(The same. An instant later.)

JIM  
(Enters hurriedly) Did you give John three neckties?

LE PÈRE  
Yes; three old ones I didn't want.

JIM  
Have you got three old ones now?

(Bill enters hurriedly.)

BILL  
Say, Mr. Man, I want some neckties.

LE PÈRE  
I just gave John all I could spare. He'll let you wear those.

JIM  
I want three neckties that belongs to me.

LE PÈRE  
Little boys don't wear neckties.

BILL  
Yes, they do, Mr. Man. I wear them, I think.

LE PÈRE  
When?

BILL  
When I go to school, I think.

JIM  
Oh! School yourself! You don't go to school.

BILL  
Yes, I do, Mr. Jimmy. I go to school every night.

JIM  
Oh, boys don't go to school at night. Big mans either one.

BILL  
I go to school, Mr. I go to school to Philadelphia every night, that's where I go.

LE PÈRE  
Chase along out now till I get through dressing.

JIM  
We won't go till you give us our neckties.

BILL  
And if you don't give us our neckties, I'll kill you, I think.

(Le Père gives them six neckties.)

## ACT 3
(The same. A few minutes later.)

LE PÈRE  
(Calling) John, will you please come here a minute and bring some of those neckties? (John comes here a minute and brings his three neckties.) Would you mind letting me have one to wear downtown?

JOHN  
(Picks out the eldest) WIll you bring it back, daddy?
