Scene—Dining room in Dr. Brown's home. The table set for breakfast. On a small table near the wall is a telephone. Just before the curtain rises, the bell is heard ringing and Mrs. Brown is answering the phone.

MRS. BROWN  
Yes. I'll call him (She goes to a door and calls) Jake! Telephone.

(After a moment's pause Dr. Brown enters. He is fully dressed in clothes that have evidently been slept in. He goes to the phone.)

DR. BROWN  
This is Dr. Brown.

VOICE AT OTHER END  
O, Dr. Brown, this is Mrs. Gordon. My baby has a terrible cough and his head's stopped up and I think he's got a fever.

DR. BROWN  
I'll drop in in a little while. (He hangs up.) Well, I guess I'll go back to bed.

MRS. BROWN  
Back to bed! Why it's a quarter to 5. You've slept forty minutes. Your breakfast's all ready.

DR. BROWN  
I had no idea it was so late. I'll— (The phone rings and he answers it.) This is Dr. Brown.

VOICE AT OTHER END  
This is Mrs. Gordon again. My baby's still sick.

DR. BROWN  
All right, Mrs. Gordon. I'll be over presently. (He hangs up.)

MRS. BROWN  
East your sausage before it gets cold.

DR. BROWN  
Um! Nice country sausage. (The phone rings. He answers it.) This is Dr. Brown.

V. AT O. E.  
Dr. Brown, this is Mrs. Parker. Signe just woke up with a high fever and she's got a bad cough and feels terribly weak.

DR. BROWN  
All right. I'll be over in a few minutes. (He hangs up. The phone rings. He answers it.) This is Dr. Brown.

V. AT O. E.  
Dr. Brown, this is Mrs. Russell again. Harold wouldn't drink the milk.

DR. BROWN  
Well, don't force it on him. I'll be over in a few minutes. (He hangs up and sits down at the breakfast table.) This looks— (The phone rings. He answers it.) This is Dr. Brown.

V. AT O. E.  
This is Mrs. Parker again. Signe's still sick.

DR. BROWN  
All right. I'll be up there pretty soon. (He hangs up. He sits down at the breakfast table.) This looks— (The phone rings. He answers it.) This is Dr. Brown.

V. AT O. E.  
This is Mrs. Russell again. Harold just drank half the milk and he doesn't seem a bit better.

DR. BROWN  
Give him the rest of it. I'll be there in a few minutes. (He hangs up and resumes his seat at the breakfast table.) This looks like— (The phone rings. He answers it.) This is Dr. Brown.

V. AT O. E.  
O, Dr. Brown. This is Joe Lewis. The wife went to bed sick last night and she's worse this morning. She's got a temperature and a cough and feels weak.

DR. BROWN  
I'll be over presently. (He hangs up and sits down at the breakfast table.) This looks like the— (The phone rings. He answers it.) This is Dr. Brown.

V. AT O. E.  
This is Mrs. Russell again. Harold finished his milk and now he's coughing.

DR. BROWN  
I'll be there presently. (He hangs up and sits down at the breakfast table.) This looks like the same piece of sausage that was here yesterday. (The phone rings. He answers it.) This is Dr. Brown.

V. AT O. E.  
This is Mrs. Parker. Signe doesn't seem to get any better and there'll be nobody to cook Pete's breakfast.

DR. BROWN  
I'll be over right away. (He hangs up and sits down at the breakfast table.) This looks like the same piece of sausage that was here yesterday.

MRS. BROWN  
Of course, it does. It is. It's the same piece that was here Christmas week.

DR. BROWN  
Well, I'll try to get rid of it. (He takes up a fork.)

MRS. BROWN  
(Looking at the clock) Great heavens! Do you know it's 5 i'clock and after!

DR. BROWN  
(jumping up from the table) I'll have to run! (He starts for the hall.) Wrap up that piece of sausage.

MRS. BROWN  
Have you got everything? Got a city directory?

DR. BROWN  
Yes, everything.

MRS. BROWN  
Where can I find you if I want you?

DR. BROWN  
In the 600 block on Wayne street for the next half hour. The 700 block the half hour after that. And so on. And call the drug store and tell them to make up 14,000 No. 876A Prescriptions. 'Bye.

(She hands him a kiss and the sausage wrapped up. He starts for the front door. The phone rings. Mrs. Brown answers it.)

MRS. BROWN  
(To Dr. Brown) It's Mrs. Parker. She says Signe's still sick. She—

Dr. Brown  
Tell her I'm bringing Pete's breakfast with me. (He goes out.)
