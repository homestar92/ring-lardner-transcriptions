# Moving Day

## Characters Represented
The Mister, a Husband and Father.  
The Missus, a Wife and Mother.  
The Baby, a Baby.

### Scene One and Only
MISTER  
Where shall we put these?

MISSUS  
On top of the other books and then a rug on top of them.

BABY  
My book.

MISSUS  
No, no. Don't touch those.

MISTER  
Get away from here.

BABY  
Ah, sit here.

MISTER  
No, no. You can't sit there. Go out on the front porch and play with Duke.

BABY  
Play with Duke.

MISSUS  
Where shall we put Duke?

MISTER  
On top of this barrel.

BABY  
Duke top of barrel.

MISTER  
Get away. Daddy said get away. You must play with baby doll.

BABY  
Play with Duke on porch.

MISSUS  
No, that's all off. Play with Kitty.

BABY  
Me play with Kitty.

MISTER  
Where shall we put Kitty?

MISSUS  
Is there room in the trunk?

BABY  
Room in tunk.

---

MISTER  
Say good-by to Kitty, babe.

BABY  
Goo-by, Kitty. W'ere is Kitty?

MISSUS  
You'll see Kitty in a few days.

BABY  
See Kitty.

MISTER  
In a few days.

BABY  
Foo days.

MISSUS  
You can't sit there.

BABY  
Can't sit dere.

MISSUS  
Why don't you go on the porch and play with baby doll?

BABY  
No‑o‑o‑o‑o. Sit dere.

MISTER  
Go away.

BABY  
No‑o‑o‑o.

MISSUS  
You're in daddy's way.

BABY  
Daddy's way.

MISTER  
Yes. Get up. Go out on the front porch.

BABY  
Play f'ont porch baby doll.

MISSUS  
No. Baby doll's all gone.

BABY  
Baby doll a' gone. W'ere Baby doll?

---

MISTER  
You mustn't sit there. Go out in the kitchen and play with horsey.

BABY  
Sit here. Read book.

MISSUS  
Books all gone.

BABY  
W'ere books?

MISTER  
Why don't you go and find your ball?

BABY  
We're ball? Ball a' gone. W'ere ball?

MISSUS  
You mustn't sit there.

MISTER  
No, no, baby. Get away.

BABY  
Get away.

MISTER  
Here, here. You can't stand there.

BABY  
Stand dere.

MISSUS  
No, you mustn't stand there. Go away, baby.

BABY  
Go 'way, baby.

MISTER  
Here. DOn't stand on that. You might break it. Bad boy!

BABY  
Bad boy. Stand on that.

MISSUS  
You mustn't.

BABY  
Mustn't stand that.

---

MISTER  
Daddy'll have to shut you up.

BABY  
Daddy, shut up.

MISSUS  
O, look! You've broken that dish. Bad boy!

BABY  
Bad boy. Boke dish.

MISTER  
You ought to be ashamed of yourself.

BABY  
Shame se'f.

MISSUS  
Why don't you go in the kitchen and play with spoons?

BABY  
Play spooons.

MISTER  
Here, here. Get away. Daddy's working. Go away. Got out on the porch and see horsey.

BABY  
See horsey. Play horsey.

MISSUS  
Don't stand on that book.

BABY  
Stand book.

MISTER  
Mother's packing that book.

BABY  
Read a book.

MISSUS  
You can read the book after a while.

BABY  
Read a book.

MISTER  
After awhile.

BABY  
Read a book.

MISSUS  
Please, please, baby. Don't stand there.

BABY  
Read a book. Stand dere.

MISTER  
Read a book and stand there after awhile.

BABY  
Read a book.

MISSUS  
TOmorrow you can read a book.

BABY  
Read a book. Stand dere. After 'while. 'Morrow.

---

MISTER  
O, get away from here.

BABY  
Read book. Get 'way from here. Stand dere. Read book. After 'while. 'Morrow.

MISSUS  
O, please, baby. Mother just fixed that.

BABY  
Fix dat. Fix dat. Read a book. 'Morrow. Fix dat.

MISTER  
No, no, no. You mustn't do that.

BABY  
Do date. Read a book. This here. Dere. 'Morrow. Baby doll. Kitty. See dat.

MISSUS  
O, I do wish you would go out on the porch and look at horseys.

BABY  
No, no. No‑o‑o‑o‑o. Stay and read a book. Dere.

MISTER  
Listen, baby. If you'll go out on the porch and look at the horseys, Daddy'll read all about "All Aboard, Conductor Man" in a little while.

BABY  
All gourd. 'Ductor man, now.

MISSUS  
No, no. You mustn't sit there.

BABY  
(Aside.) Wyinell should we move anyway. I always had a good time here.
