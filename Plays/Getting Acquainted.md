## Characters
Mr. Plant.  
Mr. Hoffman.  
Me.  
(A Loophole, forenoon. MR. PLANT and ME are standing, talking. MR. HOFFMAN comes in and walks up to MR. PLANT.)

MR. HOFFMAN  
Hello, George.

MR. PLANT  
Well, if it isn't the coroner!

ME  
(aside) Well!

MR. PLANT  
How are you, Peter?

ME  
(aside) Peter!

MR. PLANT  
Haven't you two met? This is Coroner Hoffman.

MR. HOFFMAN  
I don't believe I've had the pleasure.

ME  
Pleasure!

MR. PLANT  
I thought you'd met before.

MR. HOFFMAN  
No, I never forget a face.

(MR. PLANT exits.)

ME  
(after a pause) Never?

MR. HOFFMAN  
No, I forget their names sometimes.

ME  
(after an awkward pause) Whose names?

MR. HOFFMAN  
Oh, anybody's.

ME  
Not just clients.

MR. HOFFMAN  
Well, you look pretty well.

ME  
There's nothing at all the matter with me.

MR. HOFFMAN  
(after a pause) Well, that's good. So many have been sick.

ME  
I'm all right.

MR. HOFFMAN  
(after a pause) That's good.

ME  
I'm all right.

MR. HOFFMAN  
(after a pause) Do you live here in the city?

ME  
Yes, my name's in the telephone book—address and everything.

MR. HOFFMAN  
I'm out in Des Plaines.

ME  
Oh, 'way out there?

MR. HOFFMAN  
Oh, yes, but I can get in quick.

ME  
Well, that's good.

MR. HOFFMAN  
(after an awkward pause) Well, will you have a cigar or something?

ME  
Well, no. I don't feel very well.

MR. HOFFMAN  
Well, I hope I'll never see you again.
