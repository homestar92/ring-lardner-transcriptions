JIM

---

LE PÈRE  
Why are you afraid of him, Bill?

BILL  
Because I sat up all night and now I've got my foot on the table.

LA MÈRE  
Well, that's a naughty thing to do.

BILL  
But I think it's a nice thing to do. And policemens think it's a nice thing to do. Why are you looking at me, Mr. Daddy?

LE PÈRE  
O, I like to look at you.

BILL  
You're a good man, Mr. Daddy.

JIM  
Will you give me two pennies, daddy?

LE PÈRE  
What do you want them for?

JIM  
I want to save them till I'm five years.

JOHN  
Then what'll you buy with them?

JIM  
I'll think till I'm five years old then I'll tell you.

---

BILL

JIM

BILL  
No, because the policemens would get him. They would jump out of the patrol wagon.

JIM

BILL

JIM  
Yes, bees if they didn't, the robbers might have keys to come up and unlock us.
