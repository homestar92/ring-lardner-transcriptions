# The Last Four Innings

## Part I

### Persons Represented
Jennings, a Manager.  
Burke, his Assistant.  
Bush, a Shortstop.  
Purtell, a Third Baseman.  
High, Cobb's Understudy.  
Crawford, an Outfielder.  
Veach, an Outfielder.  
Kavanaugh, a Second Baseman.  
Burns, a First Baseman.  
Stanage, a Catcher.  
Dubuc, a Pitcher.  
Vitt, a Sub.  
Heilman, a Sub.  
Tuthill, a Trainer.  
Moriarty, a Crippled Third Baseman.  
Us, a Reporter.  
Also Umpires Connolly and Dinneen, a few White Sox, a bat boy, etc.

(Argument: US, having business with MORIARTY and arriving at the park late, seek him on the Detroit bench. The business tranacted, US sits on the bench at Moriarty's invitation.)

US  
Tommy Connolly's Li'ble to look over here and see me.

VITT  
Who? Him? He couldn't see half way here.

KAVANAUGH  
If he could see he wouldn't be umpirin'.

US  
What inning is it?

MORIARTY  
The sixth. Nothin' and nothin'.

KAVANAUGH  
We'll get him this time.

TUTHILL  
Where'd they pick that guy up?

VITT  
Who? Faber? Des Moines or somewheres.

TUTHILL  
The lucky stiffs. Every pitcher they get a hold of's got the stuff.

HEILMAN  
He's got somethin' in there today all right.

KAVANAUGH  
Has he got somethin'? Say, he give me a splitter and a curve and a fast one and they all fooled me.

VITT  
He must be pretty good.

KAVANAUGH  
What are you tryin' to do, kid somebody?

BURKE  
Never mind. We'll get him.

CHORUS  
Sure we'll get him. Go on there, Donie. Give us a start.

(BUSH pops a fly to WEAVER and returns to the bench.)

---

BUSH  
I hit under it.

KAVANAUGH  
What'd you do that for?

BUSH  
I hit under it.

KAVANAUGH  
I seen you hit under it. What'd you do it for?

BUSH  
What are you tryin' to do, kid somebody?

KAVANAUGH  
What are you crabbin' about?

BUSH  
I says I hit under it and you ast me what did I do it for.

KAVANAUGH  
I didn't mean nothin'

BUSH  
Well, you don't want to be always tryin' to kid somebody. That ain't no way to do. You should ought to be tryin' to encourage everybody.

KAVANAUGH  
I didn't mean nothin'.

JENNINGS  
Cut it out.

TUTHILL  
Get on there, Bill! Start somethin'!

KAVANAUGH  
Get on there, Bill!

BUSH  
That's better. Be tryin' to encourage somebody instead o' kiddin' somebody.

KAVANAUGH  
I didn't mean nothin'.

BUSH  
Maybe you didn't but still and all you should not ought to be kiddin'. I says I hit under that one and you ast me why did I do it. I did it because I couldn't help it, and if I could have helped it I wouldn't have done it, so you didn't have no license to kid me.

JENNINGS  
Cut it out.

(PURTELL is retired at first base.)

BURKE  
Get on there, Lefty. We'll get 'em with two out.

VITT  
Any way to get on, Lefty.

JENNINGS  
You can do it, Lefty. Yattaboy!

(HIGH sends a warm single to center field.)

JENNINGS  
Yattaboy, Lefty!

VITT  
Yattaboy, Lefty! That'd been a hit in any league.

BURKE  
A regular hit. Lefty, ol' boy.

JENNINGS  
NOW, you SAM!

BURKE  
Get a hold o' one, Sam. You can do it.

BUSH  
Get 'em down, Connolly! Open your eyes!

JENNINGS  
Over his head! Get 'em down!

VITT  
Umpire right or get out o' there.

BUSH  
Don't be lookin' over here. It won't do you no good. You can't see this far.

KAVANAUGH  
Call 'em right, you A. P. A.

(CRAWFORD flies out and the Tigers start form the bench.)

---

BUSH  
(en route) Call 'em right, Connolly. Why don't you turn your back? You could see just as well.

KAVANAUGH  
(ditto) Why don't you forfeit the game to 'em, you blind stiff?

JENNINGS  
Work hard, Jean.

(BRETON files out to VEACH)

BURKE  
Nice work, Bob.

VITT  
That's gettin' 'em, Bob. That's pitchin', Jean.

JENNINGS  
Work hard, Jean. One out.

(Faber doubles to right center.)

VITT  
You lucky stiff! What did you do, shut your eyes?

TUTHILL  
I bet that's his first hit this year.

HEILMAN  
He hit that one all right.

VITT  
The lucky stiff! I bet he don't get another base hit all season.

JENNINGS  
Never mind that, Jean. Work hard.

(WEAVER singles sharply to right, FABER going to third.)

WEAVER  
(on first base) Did you see that, Oscar? I guess I missed that one. I guess I can't hit.

VITT  
You're hittin' .189. ANybody can hit .189.

WEAVER  
They can't if they set on the bench all the time.

VITT  
(Cut out by board of censorship.)

---

TUTHILL  
What do you do, give yourself three cheers every time you hit one?

JENNINGS  
Work hard, Jean. One out. Work hard, boy.

VITT  
Anybody can hit .189.

BURKE  
No wonder you go crazy when you hit one. You don't do it enough to get used to it.

WEAVER  
I guess I hit that one, didn't I?

(BLACKBURNE hits a fast one to the ground to BUSH, whose toss to KAVANAUGH forces WEAVER. A double play seems certain, but WEAVER adroitly gets in KAVANAUGH's way, forcing him to throw badly. BURNS makes a great try and catches the ball, but UMPIRE DINNEEN rules that his foot is off the bag. BLACKBURNE is called safe and FABER's run counts.)

BURKE  
(Cut out by board of censorship.)

JENNINGS  
He was not off the bag. He was on the bag. He was not off the bag. Give us a square deal, Bill.

VITT  
(Cut out by the board of censorship.)

JENNINGS  
He interfered with him, Bill. Get your eyes open.

BURKE  
Weaver bumped him, Bill. Wake up and watch the game you (cut out by board of censorship.)

VITT  
Weaver run right into him. Can't you see nothin'? Wake up, you big stiff. Why don't you give 'em the game? Are you going to let them get away with that?

(One minute, please, to change films.)
