# Getting the Dope

## Characters
John Jones, a Young Pitcher.  
John Smith, a Young Pitcher.  
H. Oswald Barlow, a Reporter.  
T. Wilson Barrett, a Reporter.  
Scene—Tampa, Fla.

## Scene 1.

(The hotel lobby. It is evening. BARRETT and BARLOW meet at the desk.)

BARLOW  
Sent any mail stuff yet?

BARRETT  
No, but I've got to tet some away tomorrow.

BARLOW  
What are you going to hand them?

BARRETT  
O, the boss wants brief sketches of all the youngsters.

BARLOW  
So does mine. Let's split it up.

BARRETT  
All right. Who shall we start on?

BARLOW  
You mean, Whom shall we start on?

BARRETT  
All right. I'll tell you—you get the dop on Smith and I'll take Jones.

BARLOW  
All right. Which ones are they?

BARRETT  
They're in the writing room. The boob with the soft shirt and the three days' growth is Smith. The light haired guy with the teeth sticking out is Jones.

BARLOW  
Well, let's go to it.

(Exeunt.)

## Scene 2.

(A part of the Writing Room. SMITH is seated at a desk gnawing a pen and trying to remove the ink from his fingers with a blotter. Enter BARLOW.)

BARLOW  
Is this Mr. SMith?

SMITH  
Yes. Whadd'ya want?

BARLOW  
I'm Barlow of the Chicago Mail, Mr. Smith. I want to get some dope about you.

SMITH  
Aw, they ain't no dope about me.

BARLOW  
Let's see. You were with the Racine club last year?

SMITH  
Uh-huh.

BARLOW  
What was your record up there?

SMITH  
Aw, I didn't pay no attention to no record.

BARLOW  
How long have you been playing ball?

SMITH  
Aw, I dunno.

BARLOW  
You're a left-hander, aren't you?

SMITH  
Sure. Wotinell did ya think I am?

BARLOW  
How did you happen to start playing league ball?

SMITH  
Aw, I dunno. I just started, that's all.

BARLOW  
Well, much obliged to you, Mr. Smith

SMITH  
Sure. That's all right.

## Scene 3.

(Another part of the Writing Room. JONES is seated at a desk gnawing a pen and trying to remove hte ink from his fingers with a blotter. Enter BARRETT.)

BARRETT  
This is Mr. Jones, isn't it?

JONES  
Yeh.

BARRETT  
I'm Barrett of the Chicago Globe. The paper wants some stuff about you.

JONES  
Aw, I don't want you to say nothin' about me in the paper.

BARRETT  
O, we have to write something about all of you. You came here in the draft from Kewanee, didn't you?

JONES  
I guess so. I don't know nothin' about it.

BARRETT  
You're right-handed, aren't you?

JONES  
Do you mean, Am I right-handed?

BARRETT  
Yes.

JONES  
Sure, I am. Wotinell did ya think I was?

BARRETT  
Was last year your first in league baseball?

JONES  
I dunno. Is that all you want?

BARRETT  
Yes, that'll do. And I'm much obliged to you.

JONES  
Sure. Please to meetcha.

## Scene 4.

(A Room in the Hotel. BARLOW is seated at a typewriter. Enter BARRETT.)

BARRETT  
What are you doing? Getting some of your mail stuff off?

BARLOW  
Yes. Giving them the dope on Smith.

BARRETT  
What kind of a boob is he?

BARLOW  
A nut. Did you get anything on Jones?

BARRETT  
Yes. He's a rum right. Get a pencil and I'll give you what I've got.

BARLOW  
All right.

(They compare notes.)

## Scene 5.

(Another Room in the Hotel. JONES is seated on a bed, counting his money. Enter SMITH.)

SMITH  
One o' them reporters just tried to pump me.

JONES  
One of 'em was after me a while ago. He didn't learn nothin'.

SMITH  
I should say not. I wouldn't give none o' them boobs no infumation.

JONES  
He ast me my whole fam'ly hist'ry. I told him to beat it or I'd knock his block off.

SMITH  
They got a lot o' nerve.

## Scene 6.

(The Tampa Ball Park. A week has elapsed. JONES is sitting on the ground, resting. SMITH approaches him.)

SMITH  
One o' the fellahs got some Chicago papers and I was just lookin' 'em over. When I see that reporter I'm goin' to take a punch at him.

JONES  
What's the matter?

SMITH  
They's a piece in there sayin' I ain't got no speed, but I got a curve ball. And it says I only been in baseball a year and prob'ly'll need more experience. And it says I wouldn't win no prize at no beauty show.

JONES  
You oughta punch that guy's nose.

SMITH  
I'm goin' to. But you ought to of saw what they says about you.

JONES  
What?

SMITH  
They says you was light ofr a big league pitcher and that you acted like you had a sore arm and that you was with Kewanee last year.

JONES  
I wasn't no such thing. I was with the Canton club. Too light, eh? Sore arm, eh? I'll show them boobs. I'll take a wallop at that guy the first time I see him.

SMITH  
I'm goin to punch the guy that wrote that stuff about me. You can bet your dough on that.

JONES  
Jiggers! Here they come now.

(BARRETT and BARLOW stroll across the field toward JONES and SMITH.)

BARLOW  
Hello there, Smitty.

BARRETT  
Hello, Jonesey.

JONES AND SMITH  
Hello, fellahs.

BARLOW  
Been working hard?

SMITH  
O, I don't mind a little work.

BARRETT  
Hank said your arm was sore, Jonesey. How does it feel?

JONES  
O, it was sore, but it feels pretty good now. How's tricks?

BARLOW  
Well, we've got to go over and see Hank. So long, fellahs.

JONES AND SMITH  
So long, fellahs.

BARRETT  
See you later.

(BARRETT and BARLOW pass on.)

SMITH  
I'm goin' to take a pinch at that guy some day.

JONES  
I'm goin' to wallop that other guy good when I get a chance.
