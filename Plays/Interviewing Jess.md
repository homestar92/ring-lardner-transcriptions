## Characters
Jess  Willard, a champion.  
Mr. Spiro, a picture man.  
Sam Hall, a sport writer.  
Me, another sport writer.  
The proprietor.  
People Jess has met, etc.

## Scene 1
(A table in a Loop Café, Me and Sam seated thereat.)

ME  
There's Jess over at that table.

SAM  
Let's go see him. Maybe we can get a story.

## Scene 2
(Jess' table in the same. Jess and Mr. Spiro seated thereat.)

(Enter Me and Sam)

JESS  
Hello, there. Sit down and have a bite. (Me and Sam sit down.) This is Mr. Spiro. This is Mr. Hall and Mr. Lardner.

(Me and Sam and Mr. Spiro shake hands.)

SAM  
Well, Jess, you're looking—

(Mr. Fluh comes up, leans over Jess' chair and offers his hand.)

MR. FLUH  
I don't suppose you remember me. My name is Fluh. We was together at Rochester.

JESS  
Oh, yes. (Shakes hands with Mr. Fluh.) I'd like to introduce—

MR. FLUH  
I just wanted to shake hands and say good luck.

JESS  
Well, good night, Mr. Umph.

(Mr. Ump-Fluh exits.)

SAM  
Well, Jess. You're looking—

(Mr. Kloob comes up, leans over Jess' chair and offers his hand.)

MR. KLOOB  
I don't suppose you remember me, Jess. My name is Kloob. I seen you that day in Omaha.

JESS  
Oh, yes. (shakes hands with Mr. Kloob.) Do you know these boys?

MR. KLOOB  
I just wanted to shake your hand and say I hope you trim him.

JESS  
Well, thanks, Mr. Chute. Good night.

(Mr. Kloob-Chute exits.)

SAM  
I was just saying you look—

(Mr. Oof comes up, leans over Jess' chair, and offers his hand.)

MR. OOF  
I don't know if you know me or not, big fella. My name is Oof. I and you met at Cedar Rapids. I just wanted to know if you was goin' to give that bird a lickin'.

JESS  
Well, I'll try. Shake hands with my—

MR. OOF  
Well, I just wanted to shake your hand and glad to of seen you again.

JESS  
Well, good night, Mr. Wah.

(Mr. Oof-Wah exits.)

SAM  
I was just saying you—

(Mr. Spluh comes up, leans over Jess' chair, and offers his hand.)

MR. SPLUH  
I don't suppose you know me, Jess. My name's Spluh. We was together at Youngstown.

JESS  
Oh, yes. (Shakes hands with Mr. Spluh.) Meet my friends—

MR. SPLUH  
I just wanted to shake your hand. I hope you knock him out, big fella.

JESS  
Thanks. Good night, Mr. Shuffle.

(Mr. Spluh-Shuffle shuffles out.)

SAM  
Jess, you look fine.

JESS  
I feel fine.

THE PROPRIETOR  
Boys, it's 1 o'clock.
