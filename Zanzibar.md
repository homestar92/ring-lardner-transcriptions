(Enter Uddu, attired as chef.)

UDDU  
Well, here I am, former High and Mighty Prime Minister, brought down to the lowest position in the palace, receiving orders from pages and servants. What did I know about cooking? But I am improving since I spent a quarter on this. (Produces Tried and True cook book.) I've learned how.to make all kinds of delicacies, from fudges to chop suey. It took me a long time to learn some of them. The chop suey is very complicated. (Reads) Tie a cat's eyelash to two rats' tails. Squeeze the juice of one raspberry into a tub of suds and mix the suey with a squee gee spoon. I can't say that I admire the new Sultan's way of doing business, but I'm getting old and I won't have to be here long.
