# Gloom

## Cast of Characters
Kid Gleason, Assistant Manager.  
Buck Weaver, a Captain.  
Scene—A Hotel in Washington

GLEASON  
Well, Buck, you're captain now.

WEAVER  
Yes.

GLEASON  
What are you goin' to do tonight?

WEAVER  
I don't know.

GLEASON  
What shall we do?

WEAVER  
Howinell do I know?

GLEASON  
Want to play a little cards?

WEAVER  
Sure, I'll play a little.

GLEASON  
Who shall we get?

WEAVER  
I don't know. They've all gone out but Ping and I and you and Joe and Lena.

GLEASON  
Well, that's enough to start a game.

WEAVER  
Well, go ahead and start it.

GLEASON  
No, you go ahead and start it.

WEAVER  
No, you.

GLEASON  
No, you.

WEAVER  
There, Ping and Joe's went out.

GLEASON  
There goes Lena, too.

WEAVER  
Well, what shall we do?

GLEASON  
Let's go to a nickel show.

WEAVER  
All right.

GLEASON  
If he'd of stuck, they'd of been a game organized before supper.

WEAVER  
Sure, but they ain't nobody else that has sense enough to get the bunch together.

GLEASON  
Well, let's go to a nickel show.

WEAVER  
All right. I'm with you.

(They start out.)

GLEASON  
And five months more to go.

WEAVER  
'Ell.
