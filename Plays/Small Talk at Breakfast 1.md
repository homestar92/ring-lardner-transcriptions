# Small Talk at Breakfast

## Cast of Characters
John  
Jim  
Their Dad

JIM  
Is that your paper?

THEIR DAD   
Yes.

JIM  
Who paper is it?

T. D.  
My paper.

JOHN  
Who makes the paper?

T. D.  
Lots of people.

JOHN  
Does God make the paper?

T. D.  
I don't think so.

JIM  
Is God outdoors?

T. D.  
Yes.

JOHN  
Are giants outdoors?

T. D.  
Yes.

JOHN  
How old are giants?

T. D.  
A thousand years.

JOHN  
How old are they when they're born?

T. D.  
A hundred years.

JOHN  
And when they're as old as I am, are they a thousand years? Are they big?

T. D.  
Yes.

JIM  
Oh, yah.

JOHN  
I can count to a thousand from one.

T. D.  
I believe you.

JIM  
I can count, too.

JOHN  
Listen! Jim says he can count. He can't count.

JIM  
I CAN!

John  
You can't; you're too little.

JIM  
I AM NOT.

T. D.  
Never mind. You can both count.

JIM  
I'm big.

T. D.  
Sure you are.

JOHN  
How do pigs eat?

(T. D. gives an imitation of pigs at their victuals.)

JOHN  
Is that the way?

T. D.  
Yes.

JIM  
Oh, yah. And how does horseys eat?

(Another imitation.)

JIM  
Oh, yah. Dat's the way.

JOHN  
How do donkeys eat?

T. D.  
Just like horses.

JIM  
How does monkeys eat?

T. D.  
Just like donkeys.

JOHN  
THEY DO NOT!

T. D.  
No, no. You're right. This is how.

(Imitation of a monkey eating.)

JOHN  
Will you read me after breakfast?

T. D.  
I have to work.

JIM  
Where is your work?

T. D.  
In the other room?

JIM  
Go and work.

T. D.  
I will when I get ready.

JIM  
Work now.

T. D.. 
I'm not ready.

JOHN  
Do all big men work?

T. D.  
No.

JOHN  
Why do you work?

T. D.  
So you can eat.

JIM  
How does mices eat?

(Another imitation.)

JIM  
Oh, yah. Do mices' daddies work?

T. D.  
Yes.

JOHN  
Do big men have chicken pox?

T. D.  
No.

JOHN  
Will you put it in the paper that I've got chicken pox?

T. D.  
I have put it in.

JOHN  
Do Lester and Sterling know I've got chicken pox?

T. D.  
Yes, if they read the paper.

JOHN  
They can't read.

JIM  
I can read.

JOHN  
Listen! Jim says he can read.

JIM  
I CAN read.

JOHN  
You can't!

JIM  
I CAN!

JOHN  
You're too little.

JIM  
I AM NOT!

(Exit T. D.)
