## Characters.
Me.  
George, a regular.  
Twenty-one other caucasians.  
Nine hundred Africans.  
A Vendor.  
Place—Right field seats at Comiskey park.  
Time—Wednesday afternoon.

A VENDOR  
All the hist'ries of the White Sox players. How big are they? What do they weigh? Are they married or single? ENtire past career of the White Sox. Who wants it?

FIRST AFRICAN  
Yo's wastin' you' time with me, boy. AI don't ca' nothin' fo' dere pas'. You tell me what dere goin' to do today and you got me sold.

SECOND AFRICAN  
I'se got ten dat says dere goin' to git beat.

FIRST AFRICAN  
I got you fo' ten.

---

(Mr. Schalk is at bat.)

FIRST AFRICAN  
I bets.

(Mr. Schalk files to Cobb, who is bothered by the sun and just manages to hold the ball.)

FIRST AFRICAN  
(Shouting) Hey Cobb you big ⸻ (a very unfair reference to Ty's family tree)! You putty near come missin' dat, you big ⸻!

SECOND AFRICAN  
You bettah say dat in a whispah. Dat baby can climb fences.

FIRST AFRICAN  
By de time he got ovah dis fence I'd be turning de co'nah into State Street.

---

(Mr. Faber singles.)

FIRST AFRICAN  
I bets you five he don't reach.

ME  
(To George) He's already reached.

GEORGE  
He means he won't get to second.

SECOND AFRICAN.
I bets you.

(Erickson wild pitches and Faber "reaches.")

FIRST AFRICAN  
Dere goes my po'kchops.

---

(Gandil at bat.)

FIRST AFRICAN  
I bets you he reaches.

SECOND AFRICAN  
I bets you.

(Gandil strikes out.)

SECOND AFRICAN  
He reached three times, only not fur enough.

FIRST AFRICAN  
He's the weak baby on dat team. Dat's what he is. De weak baby.

SECOND AFRICAN  
You ain't got no business bettin' on dem weak babies.

FIRST AFRICAN 
You shet yo' mouth.

---

(The game ends with the White Sox victorious.)

FIRST AFRICAN  
Well, come acrost wid dat ten.

SECOND AFRICAN  
Yessuh, and I'm still ahead on de day.

ME  
(To George) That's some gambling when they pay 30 cents to get into the park and then bet $10 on the game and five on single plays.

GEORGE  
Dollars! You watch them settle (Me watches. The Second African gives the First African a dime.)
