(Stagg Field during next Saturday's game between Gobs and Maroons. A Gob has just fumbled and a Maroon has fallen on the ball. The Teams line up for scrimmage.)

MAROON QUARTER  
14—6—33—

A MAROON BACK  
Signals!

MAROON QUARTER  
14—6—33

A MAROON BACK  
Hey, wait a minute! (They gather for a whispered conference.) Say, that's Kelly through the line.

MAROON QUARTER  
What of it?

A MAROON BACK  
He quit us two downs ago and left for Rantoul.

MAROON QUARTER  
(Goes back to his position.) All right. Signals: 17—11—3—

ANOTHER MAROON BACK  
Whoa, boy! I won't have time. I'm off for Camp Grant. (He starts off the field.)

MAROON QUARTER  
Say, tell Mr. Stagg to send us a couple of men. We can't go on with nine.

(After a delay, a substitute approaches and reports to the referee.)

MAROON QUARTER  
(to the substitute) Where's the other one?

SUBSTITUTE  
I'm all.

MAROON QUARTER  
Will you stick with us for the duration of the game?

SUBSTITUTE  
Yes.

MAROON QUARTER  
Go in at right half.

SUBSTITUTE  
Where is that?

MAROON QUARTER  
All right boys. Signals: 6—13—39—42—111—

(The ball is passed to Doane, the full back, who tries to go through the Gobs' right tackle but is sat on and laid out.)

MAROON QUARTER  
(angrily) What's the matter? Who let them through?

MAROON CENTER  
Why, the left side of our line enlisted in the marines just as I was passing.

MAROON QUARTER  
(to Doane) Get up! We need you.

DOANE  
I can't.

(MAROON QUARTER wigwags to Mr. Stagg, who speaks to the leader of the band. The band starts "The Star Spangled Banner." DOANE gets up and resumes his position.)

MAROON QUARTER  
3—16—31—142—

(The play starts and is stopped. The HEAD LINEMAN rushes out on the field and points to WELSH, the Maroon left end.)

HEAD LINEMAN  
(to Referee) This bird was way offside!

WELSH  
You're a liar! I just joined the Navy.

MAROON QUARTER  
(Looks round at his players.) One, two, three, four, five— (Approaches the captain of the Gobs) Say, do you ever play basketball?
